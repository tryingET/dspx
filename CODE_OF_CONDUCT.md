Code of Conduct
===============

We are committed to a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all.

Our Pledge
----------
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to foster an open and welcoming environment. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Standards
---------
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community

Enforcement
-----------
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers privately. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

Attribution
-----------
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
